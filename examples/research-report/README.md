# Research Report: State of AI in 2025 (Analyst Version)

Research the web and produce an evidence-based report on AI in 2025.

## Goal
Write a concise but rigorous report for technical and policy readers.

## Required Sections
1. Top 3 Technical Breakthroughs in 2025
- Explain why each is a breakthrough.
- Include practical impact and limitations.

2. Open Source vs Closed Models
- Compare capability, cost, latency, and accessibility.
- Include at least one table with concrete comparisons.

3. AI Agents in Production
- Distinguish demos from production deployments.
- Cover reliability, tool use, autonomy limits, and human oversight.

4. Regulation and Policy
- Summarize major laws/policies passed or enforced in 2025.
- Include differences across at least 2 regions (for example US/EU or EU/Asia).

5. Forecast for 2026
- Provide 3 predictions.
- Label each as `high confidence`, `medium confidence`, or `speculative`.

## Research Standards
- Minimum 8 sources.
- At least 5 primary sources (official docs, company announcements, regulation text, papers).
- Use publication dates and verify that claims match 2025 events.
- Do not rely on a single outlet for any major claim.

## Output Format
- Write to `report.md`.
- Target length: 900-1200 words.
- Use markdown headings and concise bullets where helpful.
- Add an end section: `## Sources`.
- In `## Sources`, list each source as:
  - title
  - publisher/organization
  - date
  - URL

## Quality Bar
- Every major claim should be traceable to a cited source.
- Separate observed facts from your inference.
- Explicitly note uncertainty where evidence is mixed.
